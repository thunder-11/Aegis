# Ethics and Limitations

- **No real legal work.** The AgentVersa simulation does not perform real legal work, and nothing in this repository is legal advice.
- **Output variability.** LLM output can vary between runs, so a single result may not repeat.
- **Influences on behavior.** Behavior may be shaped by prompts, scenario wording, model limitations, memory, relationships, and platform design, not only by the agent's designed traits.
- **Private reflections.** A displayed private reflection is a generated structured summary, not access to hidden chain-of-thought.
- **Risk scores.** Risk scores are simulated assessments unless separately calibrated.
- **Small sample.** Results from a small number of scenarios cannot establish production safety or professional competence.
- **Privacy.** No confidential or personally identifiable information is included in this repository.
- **Version 2.** The Version 2 design is a proposal and has not been tested unless stated otherwise.
- **Observation vs. interpretation.** I try to distinguish what was displayed from what I infer about it.
